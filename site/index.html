How it works

It’s like a QR code. No one has to scan it.

Audazzio uses inaudible micro-signaling, embedded in the audio of a broadcast or live event, to deliver content to fans’ phones the moment it matters.

  1. 01

    We embed an inaudible micro-signal in the audio of your broadcast, stream or venue sound.

  2. 02

    TV speakers or the seating bowl’s sound system carry the signal to every phone in range.

  3. 03

    Phones listening through Audazzio, in a partner’s app or the browser, hear it and load the content you chose, in under a second.
